Delete your account and data

App: Drift Analyzer · Developer: Kaan Sayın (4ES)

You can permanently delete your Drift Analyzer account and everything associated with it. There are two ways to do this.

Option 1 — Inside the app (fastest)

Open Drift Analyzer → tap your profile icon (top right) → Delete account → confirm.

Your account and all associated data are removed immediately. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — By email (if you no longer have the app)

Send us a deletion request from the email address you used to sign up. We will verify the request and delete the account.

Email a deletion request

Or write to driftanalyzer@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion request”. Requests are handled within 30 days; in practice usually within a few days.

About your subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a paid subscription. Cancel it separately in your App Store or Google Play account settings, otherwise it will keep renewing.
