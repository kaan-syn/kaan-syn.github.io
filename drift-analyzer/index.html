Common questions

My drifts are not being detected.
Mount the phone firmly and let the calibration finish before you start — the app needs the phone to stay fixed relative to the car. Also make sure location permission is granted and you have a clear view of the sky; detection needs a live GPS speed reading.
Why does the app need my location?
Speed and route recording come from GPS, and drift angle is computed from speed together with the gyroscope. Location is used only while you are actively using the app — never in the background.
My top speed looks lower than what I saw on the dashboard.
The app reports GPS-measured speed. Car speedometers usually read a few percent high by design, so a small difference is expected.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought from — open your App Store or Google Play account settings → Subscriptions. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription.
How do I delete my account?
In the app: profile icon (top right) → Delete account. Or see Delete your account if you no longer have the app installed.
