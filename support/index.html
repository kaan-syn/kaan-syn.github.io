Support

Fast answers for progress, purchases, ads, and technical problems.

Before contacting us

Restart the game, confirm that your operating system and your device's app store is up to date, and check that you have a stable network connection for ads or purchases. Include your device model, operating-system version, store platform, and a short description of what happened.

Progress and saves

ChemPour stores campaign progress locally on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing app storage may permanently erase progress. Cloud save is not currently offered.

Remove Ads purchase

The “Remove Ads” item is designed as a one-time, non-consumable purchase tied to your Google Play or Apple account. If it is missing after reinstalling, open the in-game support/monetization panel and use Restore Purchases while signed into the store account used for the original purchase.

Lab Assist video did not appear

Lab Assist offers a short video after a dead end and, once the provider confirms it was completed, adds one extra empty flask to the current board. Rewarded videos depend on network availability, regional inventory, privacy choices, and service status; if no video is ready, wait briefly and try again.

Privacy choices

When advertising is enabled, the game asks you to choose basic or personalized ads before requesting or displaying an ad. You can change this choice later in the in-game support panel. Basic ads limit personalization but do not stop all processing needed to deliver and secure ads. Read the full Privacy Policy for the information involved and the available controls.

Data access or deletion

ChemPour does not have a 4ES account. Puzzle progress and settings are stored locally and can be erased by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the game. This cannot be undone.

For a privacy request involving purchase or advertising services, email 4ES using the address below with the subject Privacy request — ChemPour. Do not send passwords, receipts containing full payment details, or identity documents unless we first explain why they are required. Some requests may need to be completed directly with Unity, Google, or Apple.
